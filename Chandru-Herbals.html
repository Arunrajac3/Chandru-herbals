Our Story

Rooted in Tradition. Made for Today’s Families.

Chandru Herbals began in 2020 with a simple belief: everyday hair and skin care should be pure, honest, and close to nature — the way traditional Indian homes prepared it.

We handcraft small-batch herbal hair oil, nalangu maavu bath powder, and pure shikakai using time-tested roots, leaves, and botanicals. No mineral oils. No harsh sulfates. No synthetic fragrances.
